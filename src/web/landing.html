For teams between calls

A 3-minute movement reset for teams stuck in back-to-back calls.

Someone types how they feel, even in Hinglish, and gets one short desk reset: seated on a call, or standing beside the desk. HR sees whether teams are using it, without ever seeing a person.

Runs in the browser · No account · Nothing you type leaves the device

Team totals, never people

  • Totals only. The team view shows how many people tried a reset and came back. No names, no individual sessions, nothing anyone typed.
  • Small groups stay hidden. Teams under 10 people are never shown, and a second team is hidden if one could be worked out by subtraction.
  • Pain is never shared. A safety stop is never counted or reported.
