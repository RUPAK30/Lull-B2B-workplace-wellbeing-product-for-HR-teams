Pilot proposal · October 2026
For HR and wellbeing leads

A six-week pilot for one of your teams

Back-to-back calls leave people stiff, flat or wound up. Sukoon is a three-minute desk reset for that moment: someone types how they feel, in English or Hinglish, does one short guided reset at their desk, and goes back to their day.

What your people get

  • Three-minute desk resets, guided step by step, in the browser. Nothing to install, no account.
  • Five resets for work moments: after calls, sitting, the post-lunch slump, stress, tired eyes. Nothing on the floor, no sweat.
  • Seated on a call, or standing at the desk, switching without losing their place.
  • Pain, dizziness or injury stops the reset and points to proper help.

What you get

  • A team view: weekly active people, who is still using it, and what each team needs most. Totals only.
  • A check-in at week 3 and a plain written report at week 6: what worked, what didn’t, and whether to continue.
  • Notes from short interviews with volunteers (anonymised).

How the six weeks run

Week 0Pick one team of 10–50 people. 15-minute kick-off. Share a link.
Weeks 1–3People use it whenever they want, as often as they like.
Week 3Check-in on the team totals. Adjust anything that isn’t working.
Weeks 4–6Short interviews with up to 5 volunteers. Final report in week 6.

How we’ll judge it PROPOSED TARGETS, AGREED WITH YOU BEFORE WE START

QuestionMeasureTarget

If the targets aren’t met, the week 6 report will say so plainly.

Privacy, in plain English

  • You see totals, never a person: no names, no individual sessions, nothing anyone typed.
  • Teams under 10 people are never shown.
  • Who reported pain is never shared with anyone.
  • People can switch off the activity log at any time.

What we need from you

  • One team and one contact person.
  • A 15-minute kick-off and a 30-minute check-in at week 3.
  • Help finding up to 5 volunteers for 20-minute interviews.
