viraam विराम
Pilot proposal · October 2026
For HR and team leads

Give one team's standup an ending, for six weeks

Viraam is the last 2½ minutes of a meeting your team already holds. The team lead pastes one link into the standup invite; at the set time everyone opens it and the same short close starts for all of them, in English, हिंदी or मराठी. HR has nothing to run.

What your people get

  • One link in the invite. No app, no account, no install.
  • 2½ minutes: stand if you can, neck, shoulders, wrists, eyes, then "When this ends, I'll first…".
  • Everyone starts together from the clock; late joiners land in the right step.
  • "Something hurts" stops it and points to help (112). Nobody is told.

What you get

  • A Friday email, 9:00 IST: days it ran and how many joined, per team. Never names.
  • A launch kit: the employee note, a channel line and a calendar hold, ready to copy.
  • A 15-minute check at week 3 and a one-page report at week 6, including "stop" if that is the honest answer.

How the six weeks run

Week 015-minute kickoff. Team code agreed. DPA signed. The team lead pastes the link.
Weeks 1–5The close runs at the end of each standup. No follow-up to employees, ever.
Week 3Check the counts. Change the time or the note if needed.
Week 6Written report: use, return, cost, recommendation. You decide.

Privacy, in plain English

  • Only three counts per team: joined, started, finished. No names, no devices.
  • Teams under 10 people are never shown.
  • What people type, and anything about pain, never leaves their screen.
  • No automatic renewal: if you do nothing at week 6, the counts are deleted.

What we need from you

  • One team (10 or more people) with a daily standup, and one sender.
  • Fifteen minutes at kickoff, week 3 and week 6.
  • No roster, HRIS, SSO or IT ticket.

Price

Free for the first six teams. After the pilot, ₹1,499 per team per month. No setup fee, no annual contract.
