Business Requirements Document: Sukoon

Final product scope for the pilot release.

BRD · Version 2.1 · Rupak Ghanpathi

1. Purpose and scope

Sukoon gives desk-heavy teams a 2½-minute guided movement break and gives HR team-level evidence of use, without identifying individuals. This BRD covers the employee app, the HR site and the pilot programme for Indian companies of 50–500 people.

2. Business problem

~30%of employees take part in company wellness programmes (Plum, 3,500+ Indian orgs, 2023)
~3%of mental-health app users still active after 30 days (JMIR 2019, 93 apps)
64%of employees fear employer health data could affect pay or promotion (Aetna survey)

HR cannot show wellbeing spend is used or useful; usage collapses after launch; tools that see individuals are avoided; budgets are under pressure and founders approve spend.

3. Stakeholders

StakeholderNeed
HR / People lead (buyer)Proof of use she can show leadership; low effort to launch
Founder / CFO (approver)Small, low-risk trial with a clear decision point
Employee (user)A quick break that fits the day; privacy
ManagersNo loss of work time; an easy way to make it normal

4. Objectives and success metrics

ObjectiveMetric / target
Adoption≥ 50% of the pilot team start a session in the first two weeks
Return use≥ 1 in 3 who finish one session use it again within a week; reminder set rate as leading indicator
Value to employeesMore "Lighter" than "Not for me" answers
SafetyEvery pain or symptom report ends the session, no exceptions
Commercial3 pilot partners; qualified conversations per 10 outreach sends

5. Functional requirements

IDRequirement
FR-1Employees answer one question, "How are you feeling right now?", by tapping a work moment or typing in English, Hinglish, Hindi or Marathi; Sukoon suggests one of five 2½-minute desk breaks (neck and shoulders, back and hips, energy, calm, eyes and wrists) with its reason and real length.
FR-2Every break is office-safe (work clothes, nothing on the floor, no sweat) and works seated or standing; breaks are guided step by step with timers, and "too much" / "on a call" or "too easy" switches posture without losing the person’s place.
FR-3Pain, injury, rehab or urgent symptoms, in English, Hinglish, Hindi or Marathi, stop or prevent a session and point to proper help; urgent symptoms point to emergency care.
FR-4After a session, employees can add a weekday reminder to their own Google, Outlook or Apple calendar, or copy a Slack /remind command.
FR-5A reminder opens Sukoon with the employee's usual session one tap away.
FR-6A team break link opens one known session for everyone in a meeting.
FR-7In a pilot (team link), only allow-listed anonymous counts are sent: session started/finished, feeling, broad need category, reminder set, came back, opened from reminder, team break joined.
FR-8Employees see every count sent ("What leaves this device"), can switch counting off and clear their local history.
FR-9HR reporting shows team totals only, hides teams under 10, shows top need category per team and cost per finished session, against targets agreed before day one.
FR-10HR site: two-sided demo, personalised links (?for, ?ref), CEO slide export, pilot calculator, launch kit with QR desk card, sample report, request forms.

6. Non-functional requirements

  • Privacy: no names, device IDs, typed text or safety data leave the device; the local app's CSP blocks all external requests.
  • Safety: fixed rules cannot be overridden by any model; physiotherapist review required before a paid pilot.
  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA target, keyboard use, reduced motion, 44px touch targets, works at 360px.
  • Simplicity: nothing to install, no accounts, runs in the browser; no AI by default.
  • Quality: automated tests for the privacy contract, Hinglish safety and core flows (98 automated tests, all passing).

7. Assumptions and constraints

  • Pilots run with one team of 10–50; pricing ₹60–120 per person per month is indicative until agreed in writing.
  • Counts are events, not people; weekly active people are approximate by design.
  • English and romanised Hinglish only; Devanagari script not yet supported.

8. Out of scope (this release)

  • Slack/Teams bots, calendar access, mood or stress scoring, streaks or leaderboards, medical advice.

9. Risks and mitigations

RiskMitigation
Low reminder opt-in leads to falling useWeek-3 check-in on reminder rate; team breaks via managers
Budget goes to insurance firstPosition alongside insurance; small pilot; no annual contract
Missed safety wordingFixed rules plus tests; physio and native-speaker review
Small benefit sizeClaim "used and liked", never productivity or health outcomes
