Business Requirements Document: Viraam

Scope for the pilot release of the team close.

BRD · Version 3.0 · Rupak Ghanpathi

1. Purpose and scope

Viraam gives a team a shared 2½-minute close at the end of a meeting it already holds, and gives HR team-level counts of use without identifying anyone. This BRD covers the close, the invite generator, the HR view and the six-week pilot for Indian companies.

2. Business problem

~30%of employees take part in company wellness programmes (Plum, 3,500+ Indian orgs, 2023)
~3%of mental-health app users still active after 30 days (JMIR 2019, 93 apps)
64%of employees fear employer health data could affect pay or promotion (Aetna survey)

Wellbeing tools depend on people remembering them, so use collapses after launch; HR can't show the spend worked; tools that see individuals are avoided. A free private break reminder already exists in Microsoft 365.

3. Stakeholders

StakeholderNeed
Team lead (adopter)Set up in a minute; no extra meeting time
HR / People lead (buyer)Proof of use; nothing to run
Founder / CFO (approver)Small, cancellable cost with a clear decision point
Employee (user)Under 3 minutes, standing or seated, nobody watching

4. Functional requirements

IDRequirement
FR-1The invite generator turns a team name and standup end time into one paste-line, Google Calendar, .ics, WhatsApp and a QR code; the close starts 3 minutes before the end time.
FR-2Everyone on a team link starts from the same wall-clock time; anyone joining late lands in the current step.
FR-3The close runs 150 seconds: stand and breathe (20 s), neck, shoulders, wrists (20 s each), eyes (20 s), plan (40 s, no clock shown), done (10 s).
FR-4The plan prompt reads "When this ends, I'll first…"; the answer stays on the device and is shown on a "Leaving with:" card with Copy.
FR-5"Something hurts" stops the close at any point and shows urgent-help guidance (112). Nothing is sent.
FR-6English, Hindi and Marathi throughout, switchable at any time.
FR-7Only three events are counted per team code: joined, started, finished. Teams under 10 are never shown.
FR-8The bare link (no team) runs a personal close with a private weekly tally kept on the device.

5. Non-functional

  • Works in any modern phone or desktop browser; no account or install; loads as one page.
  • No names, emails or device IDs collected; no cookies for counting.
  • Reduced-motion users get the close without animation.

6. Commercials and success

Free for the first six teams, then ₹1,499 per team per month. Pilot success (proposed, agreed per customer): close runs at least 3 of 5 standup days in week 6; average joined at least half the team; at least 7 in 10 finish; "Something hurts" always stops it.

7. Out of scope and open items

  • Out: individual tracking, manager dashboards of people, integrations with HRIS or SSO.
  • Open: team weekly count shown to the team (needs a backend); physiotherapist review; native-speaker review of Hindi and Marathi.
