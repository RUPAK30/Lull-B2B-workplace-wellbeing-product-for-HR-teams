Viraam: give your standup an ending

The last 2½ minutes of a meeting your team already holds. One link in the invite, everyone starts together, HR has nothing to run.

One pager · Rupak Ghanpathi
Pilot-stage · India · per team

The problem

~30%of employees take part in company wellness programmes (Plum, 3,500+ Indian orgs, 2023)
~3%of mental-health app users still active after 30 days (JMIR 2019, 93 apps)
64%of employees fear employer health data could affect pay or promotion (Aetna survey)

Wellbeing tools ask people to remember a new habit, so few use them, HR can't show they worked, and anything that feels tracked is avoided. A free break reminder already ships inside Microsoft 365, and it is private to each person.

The product

  • The meeting is the reminder. The team lead pastes one line into the standup invite; at the end time everyone opens the link and the same close starts for all.
  • 2½ minutes: stand if you can, neck, shoulders, wrists, eyes, then "When this ends, I'll first…". English, हिंदी, मराठी.
  • Safety: "Something hurts" stops it and points to help (112). Nobody is told.
  • Personal close: the bare link works alone, whenever a call was a lot; a private weekly tally stays on the device.

Privacy and HR

  • Three counts per team (joined, started, finished); no names; teams under 10 hidden.
  • A Friday email to the team lead; a week-6 report with use, return and cost per team-day.
  • No roster, HRIS, SSO or install.

Business model

Free for the first six pilot teams, then ₹1,499 per team per month. No setup fee, no annual contract.

Why it should work (graded evidence)

  • Strong: if-then plans help people start the next task (94 tests, d = .65).
  • Moderate: movement beats passive seated breaks; groups with a shared identity and goal keep exercising more (d = .74); short breaks lower fatigue but don't raise performance, so we don't claim productivity.
  • Unknown: whether teams keep it up for weeks. That is what the pilot measures.

Status

Live and pilot-ready; no customers yet. Physiotherapist and native-speaker reviews are pending before the first paid month.
