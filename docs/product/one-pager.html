Lull: wellbeing HR can prove, without watching anyone

3-minute guided movement resets for desk-heavy teams, with team-level proof for HR.

One pager · Rupak Ghanpathi
Pilot-stage, India, 50–500 person companies

The problem

~30%of employees take part in company wellness programmes (Plum, 3,500+ Indian orgs, 2023)
~3%of wellbeing-app users still active after 30 days (JMIR 2019, 93 apps)
64%of employees fear employer health data could affect pay or promotion (Aetna survey)

HR buys wellbeing tools that few people use, can't prove the spend worked, and employees avoid anything that feels tracked. Budgets are tight and the founder approves.

The product

  • Employees type how they feel, in English or Hinglish, and get one 2–4 minute guided movement session that adapts.
  • Safety first: pain or urgent symptoms stop the session and point to proper help, before or during it.
  • Coming back: opt-in reminders on their own calendar or Slack; one tap to "your usual"; team resets at the end of meetings.
  • HR sees team totals, broad need categories and a week-6 report with cost per finished session.

Privacy by design

  • Team totals only; teams under 10 hidden (and a second team if one could be worked out by subtraction).
  • One tested allow-list of anonymous counts; never names, device IDs, typed text or pain reports.
  • Each employee sees exactly what was sent, and can switch it off.
