Who you are dealing with

Sukoon is run by Rupak Ghanpathi, an individual in India. It is an early-stage product; there is no registered company name or published postal address yet. Contact: rupakr2g@gmail.com.

Using the site

You may read the site, try the demo, and share links with your team. Do not attack the site, scrape it at volume, copy it as your own, or use it to break the law.

The demo is free and needs no account. It may change or stop without notice.

Health and safety

Sukoon is a general movement break, not medical care, treatment, rehabilitation, or diagnosis. It does not replace a clinician.

Use your judgement. Stop if anything hurts, and seek medical advice for the red flags on the safety page. If you are pregnant, recovering from surgery or injury, or have an ongoing condition, check with a clinician first.

You use the movements at your own risk. To the extent the law allows, Sukoon is not liable for an injury that follows from ignoring pain, a red flag, or clinical advice.

Pilots and payment

  • The first six team pilots are free for six weeks. There is no setup fee and no automatic renewal.
  • After a pilot, the price is ₹1,499 per team per month, GST extra. Billed monthly.
  • Either side can stop with 15 days' written notice. Fees already billed are not refunded for a partial month unless we agree otherwise.
  • A signed pilot agreement and DPA set the final terms for your pilot and override this page.

There are no customers yet. The pilot is the first paid step, and only after it has run.

Data
