The workspace holds four things and nothing else: your company name, your team names, the seat count per team, and the weekly counts. There is no field for a person, an email address, an employee ID, a device ID, or a session record, so none can be stored by accident.

What is collected

Only counts leave a device. A pilot link carries a short team code. When someone starts or finishes a reset, the app adds one to that team's count. There is no row for a person and no session history.

Every event that can leave the device in a pilot
EventWhat it means
session-startedSomeone opened a reset.
session-finishedThe reset reached the end.
came-backSomeone returned on a later day.
reminder-setSomeone made their own calendar or Slack reminder.
opened-from-reminderA reset was opened from that reminder.
team-reset-joinedSomeone opened a shared team-reset link.

The count path is pilot/<team>/<event>. Nothing else is in it.

What the report contains

Per team, per week: how many started, how many finished, and how many started but did not finish. That is the four-field row: team, week, started, finished.

What is never collected

  • Names, email addresses, employee IDs, device IDs, or IP addresses in the report.
  • Individual sessions, timestamps per person, or a person's history.
  • What anyone typed, what they chose, or how they felt afterwards.
  • Anything about pain or a safety stop. Those are care, not a metric, and are never counted.
  • Location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health-app data.

There is no login, no profile, and nothing to install. Choose a team code, not a person's name.

Small numbers are suppressed

A team appears in a weekly email only when at least 10 people started that week. We also hide the next-smallest team, so a hidden team cannot be worked out by subtracting the visible rows from a company total.

We call this aggregated with small numbers suppressed. We do not call it anonymous. A team code can describe a group, and a group can be small. Suppression is what protects the group, not a promise that the data contains no person at all.

If too few teams pass the threshold, the week's email says so and shows no team rows.

Retention

  • Team counts: kept for the pilot plus 30 days, then deleted. Deleted earlier on request.
  • Pilot email: kept while the pilot and any contract run, then deleted on request.
  • Host logs: short-lived (about an hour to three days, depending on the plan). We do not export them.
  • Analytics: aggregate counts only. Raw IP addresses are not retained.
  • AI: off in every pilot. If you switch it on yourself, Anthropic keeps the text for up to 30 days and Sukoon stores nothing.

These are the intended periods. A signed DPA sets the final ones for your pilot.

AI

AI is off by default, and off in every pilot. The public demo is scripted; no text is sent anywhere.

The local build has an optional guided mode. If you run it yourself with your own API key and switch it on, the text you type goes to Anthropic to choose a reset. It is not used to train models by default and is deleted within 30 days. Sukoon does not store it.

AI never decides whether someone is safe. Safety stops are fixed rules, not a model's judgement.

DPA summary

I sign a data processing agreement before the first pilot link goes out. This is the plain-language summary.

  • Roles: your company is the data fiduciary; Sukoon is the processor.
  • Scope: team-level counts only, as listed above. No employee identifiers.
  • Purpose: running and reporting the pilot. Nothing else.
  • Subprocessors: the four listed above. Notice before any change.
  • Security: HTTPS in transit, access limited to the founder, no employee-level store to breach.
  • Deletion: on request, within 30 days, with written confirmation.
  • Breach: tell you without undue delay, and within 72 hours of becoming aware.
  • Audit: answer reasonable questions about this page and the code. No audit certificate yet.
Not yet lawyer-reviewed. This summary and the DPA are drafts written by the founder. If your legal team needs changes, send them and I will work through them before the pilot starts.

The employee notice

You send this before the link. It is short on purpose.

What this is. Sukoon is a three-minute seated movement break. It is optional. There is no login and nothing to install.

What is counted. If our team joins the pilot, Sukoon counts how many people in the team started and finished a reset each week. It does not record who.

What is not counted. What you type, how you feel, and anything about pain are never sent. If you stop or report pain, no one at the company is told.

Your choice. You can ignore the link, turn counting off in the app, and clear the device log at any time.

Questions. Ask rupakr2g@gmail.com.

This is a template, not legal advice. Your company decides the final wording.

Security today

Being small means being precise about what exists.

  • HTTPS everywhere. No accounts, passwords, or employee database.
  • Access to the deployment and email is limited to the founder.
  • No SOC 2, ISO 27001, or penetration test yet. We will not show a badge we have not earned.
  • Report a security issue to rupakr2g@gmail.com. I reply within two working days.

If your procurement process requires certifications Sukoon does not have, the honest answer is that this is not the right stage for that process yet.
