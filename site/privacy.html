What is collected

Aggregate page counts. Sukoon uses GoatCounter, a cookieless analytics tool, to see which pages are read. It sets no cookies, writes nothing to local storage, and does not keep your IP address. It counts a visit with a hash that rotates daily and is then discarded.

Short-lived host logs. The site is hosted on Vercel. Its access logs may include an IP address and are kept for about an hour to three days, depending on the plan. We do not export them and do not use them for reporting.

Nothing else. No account, no form, no advertising pixel, no social tracker, no fingerprinting, no data broker.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. That is why there is no cookie banner.

The app stores a small amount of state in your browser so a session works and so you can clear it yourself. It does not send that state to Sukoon in a pilot. The data page explains exactly what can leave a device.

If you email

If you write to rupakr2g@gmail.com, I keep the email so I can reply and run the pilot. It sits in Gmail. I do not add you to a marketing list, and I do not sell or share it.

Ask me to delete the correspondence at any time and I will, unless I need it for an active contract or a legal obligation.

Sharing

The only processors involved in this website are Vercel (hosting) and GoatCounter (aggregate counts). The full list, including pilot processors, is on the subprocessor page.

I do not sell data, and I do not share it for advertising.

Your choices

You can ask what I hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask me to delete it. Email rupakr2g@gmail.com and I will respond within 30 days.

If you are in a pilot, your employer is the data fiduciary and Sukoon is the processor. Ask them first if the request is about pilot data; I will help them answer it.

Contact

Sukoon is run by Rupak Ghanpathi in India. There is no registered company name, grievance officer, or postal address published yet; this is an early-stage product. Email is the honest route: rupakr2g@gmail.com.
