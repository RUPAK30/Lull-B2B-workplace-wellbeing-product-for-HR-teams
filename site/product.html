The user
The employee: takes a 3-minute reset between calls.
The buyer
HR: needs to show the benefit is used, without seeing a person.

How a wellbeing benefit moves through a company

Five stages, from budget to renewal. Lull plugs into each one.

  1. Budget

    Today: fixed wellness spend.

    Lull: prices per person, with a calculator.

  2. Rollout

    Today: a launch email nobody reads.

    Lull: team link, QR desk card, manager message.

  3. Daily use

    Today: usage dies after week 2.

    Lull: calendar or Slack reminders and team resets in meetings.

  4. Review

    Today: HR can't prove usage at renewal.

    Lull: week-6 report with team totals.

  5. Decision

    Today: renew on gut feel.

    Lull: continue or stop against targets agreed upfront.

Three hard decisions

Privacy
Allow-listed anonymous counts only. Teams under 10 hidden. No typed text leaves the device.
Safety
Fixed rules above the model. The model can add caution, never remove it.
Distribution
Calendar or Slack reminders and team resets instead of another app.
Privacy in detail

Allowed counts: session started and finished, feeling, broad need category, reminder set, came back, opened from reminder, team reset joined. A second team is hidden if subtraction could reveal one under 10. See data handling.

Safety in detail

Hinglish pain words trigger the same stop as English. In the eval, the word list alone missed 10 of 14 body concerns; the list plus Claude missed none.

Alternatives considered
  1. Do nothing: no proof, no risk, no cost, but no benefit either.
  2. Gym benefit: high usage among the already-active; low reach for desk-heavy teams.
  3. EAP or counselling: reactive, focused on crisis; stigma prevents uptake.
  4. Generic app: another download and login. Median 3.3% retention at 30 days (JMIR 2019, 93 apps).
  5. Calendar reminder or YouTube stretch: free, but no data for HR.
  6. Lull: between calls, 3 minutes, no install, no account, manager ritual.

Measurement and kill criteria

Usage and trust, never health, productivity or ROI. No customer results yet.

Measure
Adoption, return within a week, reminder opt-in, team reach, cost per participant.
Stop if
Under 30% try by week 2, or under 1 in 5 return within a week.
All proposed kill criteria
  • Under 30% try by week 2.
  • Under 1 in 5 return within a week.
  • Under 25% set a reminder.
  • Any unresolved safety issue.
  • HR cannot name a decision they would make differently.

Proposed criteria, not customer guarantees. Also tracked: safety stops and zero data incidents. The 64-person company is synthetic.

Research sources

Lancet Global Health 2022: about 50% of Indian adults were insufficiently active in 2022 (57% of women, 42% of men), up from 22% in 2000; projected 60% by 2030.

IJCMPH Chennai study: 69% of software professionals reported work-related musculoskeletal problems in the past year; neck 29.6%, lower back 22.9%.

PLOS ONE 2022 micro-breaks meta-analysis (22 studies): more energy and less fatigue, with no overall effect on task performance, so Lull makes no productivity claim.

Plum Health Report of Corporate India 2023 (3,500+ organisations): ~30% participation in wellness programmes; 20–30 year-olds use them about half as much as over-50s.
