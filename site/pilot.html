Questions

Do employees need to install anything?

No. It opens in a browser from one link. No app, no account, no password.

Does it connect to Slack or Teams?

Not in the pilot. People can make their own Slack reminder or add a calendar reminder; the app gives them the text. Sukoon does not read any workspace.

What does IT have to do?

Nothing. It is a normal HTTPS site. No agent, no extension, no firewall change. Your security team can read the subprocessor list.

Is this medical care or rehab?

No. It is a general movement break. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a clinician. Read safety and limits.

What if someone is in pain?

The app stops and tells them to rest and seek advice if needed. That stop is never counted and never reported to you.

What happens to the data if we stop?

It is deleted on request, within 30 days, with written confirmation. The counts are team totals, not employee records.

Does it understand everything people type?

Out of the box, Sukoon runs on fixed rules with no AI: about 900 words and phrases across English, Hinglish, Hindi and Marathi, covering how people feel, pain and emergencies, and changes mid-session. Anything it doesn't recognise gets a clear reply, never silence. If your company wants it to understand any sentence, an AI layer (such as Claude) can be switched on with your own API key and a written opt-in.
