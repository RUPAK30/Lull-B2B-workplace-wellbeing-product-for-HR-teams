How the six weeks go.

  1. Week 0Pick a team. Agree targets. Share a link.
  2. Week 3Check team totals together. Adjust.
  3. Week 6A written readout. Continue or stop.
  • We hand you the rollout.Launch email, manager message and a QR desk card, ready to send.
  • Your people getA 3-minute reset between calls, seated or standing, in English or Hinglish, with an opt-in reminder.
  • You getTeam totals: who's taking breaks and what each team needs. Never a person.

  • Under 30% try it by week 2.Or under 1 in 5 come back within a week.

Why are two teams hidden?

Both have fewer than 10 people, so neither is shown. If only one team were that small, Lull would hide the next-smallest team too. Otherwise anyone could subtract the visible teams from the company total and work out the small one.

HR saw team totals only. No names, no messages.

Questions, answered.

What if nobody uses it?
You'll know by week 3, not at renewal. People set their own reminders, and you'll see how many did.
Can HR see what someone typed?
No. Team totals only; teams under 10 are hidden. Every count, listed ›
We already have insurance wellness. Why this?
Keep it. That covers the serious moments; Lull covers an ordinary Tuesday afternoon.
Remote, field or shift teams?
Yes. It's a link on any phone or laptop, at any hour.
