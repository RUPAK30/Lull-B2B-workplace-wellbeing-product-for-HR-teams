Lull pilot pack

3 founding pilot slots. 6 weeks. One team of 10–50.

Indicative price: ₹60–120 per person per month. No annual contract.

What employees get

  • One 3-minute movement reset between calls, in English or Hinglish.
  • Five office-safe resets: neck & shoulders, back & hips, energy, calm, eyes & wrists.
  • Seated on a call or standing at the desk.
  • Safety stop: pain or injury ends the reset.
  • Opt-in reminder on their own calendar or Slack.

What HR gets

  • Team totals only: weekly active people, return rates, need categories.
  • Teams under 10 hidden; a second team hidden if subtraction could reveal one.
  • A check-in at week 3 and a plain written report at week 6.
  • No individual data, no typed messages, no pain or safety data.

What you need to do

  1. Pick one team of 10–50 people. 15-minute kick-off. Share a link.
  2. Review team totals at week 3. Adjust anything that isn't working.
  3. At week 6, receive a written readout: what worked, what didn't, and whether to continue.

Privacy and data

The demo sends nothing. A pilot link sends only the anonymous counts listed in Settings. No names, device IDs, typed text or pain/safety data leave the device.

  • GoatCounter counts events, not people.
  • The local activity log stays in the browser; unmatched messages up to 120 characters can be turned off/cleared.
  • Data deletion on request; no sale, no ads, no model training.

Targets and stop criteria

Proposed kill criteria: under 30% try by week 2; under 1 in 5 return within a week; under 25% set a reminder; any unresolved safety issue; HR cannot name a decision they'd make differently.

Next step

  1. 15-minute call → choose one team and agree targets.
  2. Launch in a week.
  3. Review week-3 check-in. Continue or stop at week 6.
