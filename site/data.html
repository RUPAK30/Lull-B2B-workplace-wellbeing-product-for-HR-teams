Data & terms

Data handling and pilot terms

What leaves a device, what never does, and the terms of a pilot, in plain language.

What we promise your employees

  • Opt-in, and invisibleNobody is signed up, and nobody can see who joined or who didn't.
  • Never used to judge anyoneIt can't be: no names, no individual records.
  • No personal dataNo names, emails or device IDs. Nothing personal to consent to or leak.
  • Small teams hiddenTeams under 10 never appear.

Designed so no personal data is collected, which keeps your obligations under India's data protection law (DPDP Act) minimal. Not legal advice.

Summary

What's sent

Anonymous counts from a pilot link, such as "session finished". Counts events, not people.

What's never sent

Names, device IDs, typed text, pain or safety data. Nothing is sold, used for ads or used to train models.

Your controls

The activity log stays in the browser. Turn counting off, clear the log, or request deletion.

Every count a pilot can send

The full allow-list. The demo, without a team link, sends nothing at all.

Pilot terms

  • ScopeOne team of 10–50 at a 50–500 person company.
  • Duration6 weeks: check-in at week 3, written readout at week 6.
  • Price₹60–120 per person per month, indicative. No annual contract.
  • Ending the pilotEither side can stop at any time.
  • Data deletionPilot data is deleted on request.
  • SafetyPain, dizziness or injury stops the reset and points to proper help.
