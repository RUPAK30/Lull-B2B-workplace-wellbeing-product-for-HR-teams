Data & terms

Data handling and pilot terms

What leaves a device, what never does, and the terms of a pilot, in plain language.

Privacy. Built in, not bolted on.

  • Collects less.Only anonymous counts, such as "a session finished". No names, emails or anything that identifies a phone or laptop.
  • Stays on the device.What people type, and their activity log, stay in their browser.*
  • You can see everything.Every count is listed below. People can turn counting off or clear their log in Settings.
  • Nothing to breach.Nothing personal is stored on a server. Pilot data is deleted on request.

Privacy facts · what Lull collects

Names or emailsNever

*Unless your company turns on the optional AI mode in writing. Pilots don't use it.

Who sees what.

Each employee
Their own activity log, on their own device. Nobody else.
HR
How many people in each team took a break. Teams under 10 stay hidden (Microsoft's workplace analytics hides groups under 5).
Rupak (the builder)
The same anonymous counts, to write the week-6 readout.
Nobody
Names, what anyone typed, pain reports, or who joined and who didn't.

India's DPDP Rules (notified November 2025) are now phasing in. Lull collects no personal data, which keeps your obligations minimal. Not legal advice.

Employee controls: the activity log stays in their browser; they can turn counting off or clear it in Settings. Pilot data is deleted on request. Nothing is sold, used for ads or used to train models.

Pilot terms

  • ScopeOne team of 10–50 at a 50–500 person company.
  • Duration6 weeks: check-in at week 3, written readout at week 6.
  • Price₹60–120 per person per month, indicative. No annual contract.
  • Ending the pilotEither side can stop at any time.
  • Data deletionPilot data is deleted on request.
  • SafetyPain, dizziness or injury stops the reset and points to proper help.
