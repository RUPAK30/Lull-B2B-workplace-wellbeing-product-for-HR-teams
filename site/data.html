Key principles

  • No names, device IDs, typed text or pain/safety data leave the device. The only events that may leave a device are the allow-listed anonymous counts above.
  • GoatCounter counts events, not people. It counts how many times an event happened, not who performed it.
  • The local activity log stays in the browser. Unmatched messages up to 120 characters can be stored locally and can be turned off or cleared in Settings.
  • The demo without a team link sends nothing. Without a ?team=sales link, no pilot counts are sent anywhere.
  • Optional Claude mode. If agreed in writing, typed messages may be sent to Anthropic. The default pilot mode is Mock: nothing is sent.
  • Data deletion on request. Anyone can request deletion of their data; there is no sale, no ads, and no model training.

Pilot terms (plain language)

TermDetail
ScopeOne team of 10–50 people at a 50–500 person company. Employees type how they feel in English or romanised Hinglish.
Duration6 weeks, with a check-in at week 3 and a written readout at week 6.
Price₹60–120 per person per month, indicative. No annual contract. Pilot ends or continues by mutual agreement.
TerminationEither party can stop the pilot at any time. Pilot data is deleted on request.
Data deletionOn request, all personal data is deleted. Aggregated team totals in HR view are anonymised.
Safety escalationPain, dizziness or injury reports stop the reset and point to proper help. Urgent symptoms point to emergency care.
