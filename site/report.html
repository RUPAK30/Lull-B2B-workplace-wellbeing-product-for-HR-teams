How this is calculated

Every number comes from six counters per team per week: starts, finishes, returns, reminders set, reminders opened, and team-reset joins. There is no person-level row.

  • Started: a reset was opened from the team link.
  • Finished: the 2:30 reached the end. A stop is not a finish.
  • Still using: at least one start in weeks 5 or 6, among people who started at least once.
  • Cost per finished session: the indicative monthly price divided by finished sessions. It is a range because the price depends on the band.
  • Safety stops: never counted, never included, never reported.

If your team has fewer than 10 starters in a week, the report says so and shows no team row. The full data map.
