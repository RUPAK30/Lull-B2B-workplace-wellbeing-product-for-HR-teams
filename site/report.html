HR sees team totals only. No names, no messages, no individual sessions. Teams under 10 are hidden, and the next-smallest team is hidden too.

Sample figures are from a made-up company. Sukoon is a pilot-stage product, not a medical or mental-health service.

What this report cannot tell you

  • It shows that people started and finished a timer, not that they exercised or felt better.
  • It can't show who used it, by design.
  • Six weeks is short. Novelty usually lifts weeks 1–2.
  • The figures here are a made-up sample company, not customer data.

How this is calculated

Every number comes from six counters per team per week: starts, finishes, returns, reminders set, reminders opened, and team-break joins. There is no person-level row.

  • Started: a break was opened from the team link.
  • Finished: the break reached the end. A stop is not a finish.
  • Came back: a start on a later day, after a finished session.
  • Cost per team-day run: the invoice for the period divided by the number of days teams actually ran the close.
  • Safety stops: never counted, never included, never reported.

If your team has fewer than 10 people taking part in a week, the report says so and shows no team row. The full data map.
