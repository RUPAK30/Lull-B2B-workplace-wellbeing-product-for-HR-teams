    The evidence
    A study of 46,336 workers found individual programmes such as apps and mindfulness showed little effect on wellbeing. In a randomised trial, the people who joined were already the healthier ones.Oxford Wellbeing Research Centre, 2024 · Illinois Workplace Wellness Study, NBER

    Quick answers

    Could this be used in performance reviews?
    No. There is nothing individual to use: HR sees team totals only.
    Is it medical care?
    No. Short movement resets; pain, dizziness or injury stops the reset and points to proper help.
    What if it doesn't work?
    You'll know by week 3, and the week-6 readout says so plainly.
