Questions, answered.

Can a manager see one person?

No. There is no screen, export, or report that shows one person. Teams under 10 are hidden, and so is the next-smallest team, so the small one can't be worked out by subtraction.

Does anyone find out who reported pain?

No. Nothing about pain, injury, or safety leaves the device. The reset stops and points the person to proper help. Nobody at your company is told, and it is never counted.

Will my team think we're monitoring them?

They can open Settings and see the exact list of what leaves their device, then switch counting off. No names, no scores, no rankings, no manager view. Here is the list they see.

Will anyone actually use it?

That is the only question worth piloting. After a session, each person can put a weekday reminder in their own calendar or Slack, and you'll see how many did. If usage stalls, you'll know by week 3, not at renewal.

Is it medical care?

No. These are three-minute desk resets, not diagnosis or therapy. If someone mentions pain, dizziness, or injury, the reset stops and points them to proper help.
