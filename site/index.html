The rules that make it safe

  • A team appears only at 10 starters. The next-smallest team is hidden too, so the small one cannot be worked out by subtraction.
  • No names, emails, employee IDs, device IDs, IP addresses in the report, or individual sessions.
  • Nothing about pain, what someone typed, or how they felt is ever counted. Those stay on the device.
  • AI is off by default. Safety stops are never reported.

We call this aggregated with small numbers suppressed. We don't call it anonymous. The exact data, the subprocessors, and the DPA.

What usually goes wrong

Most wellness benefits fail the same four ways.

Common failures, what Sukoon does, and what it costs us
FailureWhat we didWhat it costs us
Nobody opens it after week 22:30, one link, an opt-in weekday reminder on their own calendarNo streaks or badges to inflate numbers
HR has to run itA ready launch email, manager note and QR card; one Friday emailNo dashboard, no custom reports
People fear being watchedTeam totals only; teams under 10 hidden; no rosterWe can never show you who used it
Nobody can prove it workedTargets agreed on day one; a week-6 report that says "Not met" when it isn'tThe product can visibly fail

Blunt version: if your team has no company email or calendar, the reminder won't reach them, and usage will be lower.
