Why this exists, how it runs, and what it costs

Why this exists

Two good weeks, then silence. The gap is a number you can defend.

You already know the pattern: the launch email lands, the calendar fills for a fortnight, then it quietly stops. The gap is rarely the programme — it is a number you can put in front of Finance and defend. This produces that number, and a report that can recommend stopping.

How it runs · 2:30

One link, 2:30 seated, counts by Friday.

One link, pasted into the channel you already use. 2:30, seated, in work clothes, between two calls. Friday 9:00: started and finished, per team, in one email. Week 6: a two-page report, in the shape your CFO already reads.

When it starts

Live in ten minutes. First Friday email this week.

Live in ten minutes, not two weeks. Your first Friday email arrives this week. The first six teams run free; after that, monthly billing with fifteen days' notice and no setup fee.
