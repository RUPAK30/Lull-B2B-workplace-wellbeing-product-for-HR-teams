Why this exists, how it runs, and what it costs

Why this exists

Most wellness programmes are opened in week one and gone by week three, and nobody can say whether the last one worked. This one gives you a number — and a report that is allowed to say stop.

How it runs

One link, pasted into a channel. 2:30, seated, in work clothes, between two calls. Friday 9:00: started and finished, per team, in one email. Week 6: a two-page report, in the shape your CFO already reads.

When, and what it costs

Live in ten minutes, not two weeks. Free for the first six teams. Then ₹120 / ₹90 / ₹60 a person a month at 40 / 120 / 250 seats — GST extra, billed monthly, fifteen days' notice, no setup fee. The full price page →

Who sees what

HR sees

  • Team name and seat count
  • How many started
  • How many finished
  • Cost per starter

HR never sees

  • A person, an email, an employee ID
  • A session record or a device
  • Anything anyone typed
  • A safety stop

Not shown at all

  • Any team under ten starters
  • And the next-smallest team

So a small team can't be recovered by subtraction. That costs us a row most weeks.

No employee personal data is collected or stored. There is no employee record to protect, export or delete under the DPDP Act — because no field exists to put one in. The full data map →
