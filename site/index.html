    Quick answers

    Don't most wellness programmes fail? Many do. Lull doesn't claim to fix wellbeing.

    Large studies find individual programmes like apps and mindfulness show little effect on wellbeing (Oxford, 46,336 workers, 2024; Illinois randomised trial, NBER). Lull is a 3-minute break built into how teams work, including resets at the start of meetings, and it is measured honestly against targets you agree upfront.

    Could this be used in performance reviews? No: there is nothing individual to use.

    HR sees team totals only. No names, no individual sessions, nothing anyone typed. Who reported pain is never shared with anyone. The demo sends nothing; a pilot link sends only the anonymous counts listed in Settings.

    Will people use it? It lives in the workday: calendar or Slack reminders and team resets.

    After a session, each person can put their own weekday reminder in their calendar or Slack, and you'll see how many did. If usage stalls, you'll know by week 3, not at renewal.

    Is it medical care? No: short movement resets, with a safety stop for pain.

    Three-minute desk resets you can do in work clothes, seated or standing. If someone mentions pain, dizziness or injury, the reset stops and points them to proper help.

    What if it doesn't work? Targets are agreed upfront; the week-6 readout says so plainly.

    For example: at least half the team tries it, at least 1 in 3 comes back within a week. We check the team totals together at week 3. If the targets aren't met, the week 6 report will say so plainly.
