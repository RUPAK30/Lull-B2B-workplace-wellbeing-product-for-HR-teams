The whole product

Four moves. 2:30. Seated, in work clothes.

No mat, no sweat, no change of clothes. Each cue is one sentence. The timer is the only thing that moves.

Open the full reset
  1. 0:00–0:40

    Neck side release

    Sit tall and let one ear drift toward the same shoulder. Switch sides halfway.

    Small and slow beats big.
  2. 0:40–1:20

    Shoulder rolls

    Roll the shoulders up, back and down. Reverse halfway through.

    Let the shoulders get heavy.
  3. 1:20–2:00

    Chest opener

    Hold the back of your chair or clasp your hands behind you, then gently lift the chest.

    Open up after all that leaning in.
  4. 2:00–2:30

    Long exhale

    Let the breath slow down. Make each exhale a little longer than the inhale.

    Nearly there.

What reaches you

This is the whole payload.

One line per team per week. No names, no messages, no individual sessions.

{
  "team": "engineering",
  "week": 6,
  "started": 45,
  "finished": 38
}

A team appears only at 10 people, and the next-smallest team is hidden too, so the small one cannot be worked out by subtraction. There is no per-team breakdown by need or feeling. We call this aggregated with small numbers suppressed. We don't call it anonymous.

AI is off by default. If a pilot turns it on, the text someone types is read by Anthropic, deleted within 30 days, and never stored by Lull.

The exact list, and the AI exception

What is never in it

  • Names, emails, phone numbers, employee IDs
  • Anything anyone typed
  • Anything about pain, injury, dizziness, or safety
  • Device, browser, IP address, or which person

I built this because our own wellness benefit went unused.
