What week 6 adds

The weekly line answers "is the close still running". The week-6 report answers what Finance asks: did teams keep it up, what did it cost, and should we continue.

What it contains

  • Targets agreed in week 0 — and whether they were met, in those words.
  • Days run and average joined per team, with the under-10 rule applied.
  • The one number a finance team will ask for: cost per team-day run.
  • A recommendation that can say stop, and the reasons it would.
  • A box headed what this report cannot tell you — no health outcome, no individual, no benchmark, because there are no other customers yet.
